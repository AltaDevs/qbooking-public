# Q-Booking Public Roadmap

This roadmap is intentionally high level. Details may change as Q-Booking moves through controlled launch.

## Now

- Controlled-launch business onboarding
- Public booking pages
- Customer phone login and verification
- Business dashboard foundation
- Booking confirmation and notification flows
- Internal Discord-assisted business setup

## Next

- Better dashboard analytics
- Improved customer booking history
- More scheduling controls for staff availability
- More polished business profiles
- Public showcase material and demo screenshots

## Later

- Advanced WhatsApp/business-number routing
- Multi-branch support
- More advanced reporting
- Waiting list and no-show tracking
- Self-service onboarding after controlled launch feedback
